AI Speech Listening Study

You will hear 24 audio clips, each about one minute long. This study takes approximately 30 minutes.

Context: Each clip is the response of an AI assistant during a real spoken English conversation. The model, conversation topic, and timing are hidden. Your task is simply to judge how interpretable the English speech sounds.

What you will do

  • Listen to each clip using headphones in a quiet place.
  • Rate how understandable the AI's speech is on a 4-point scale.
  • Play each clip before answering. You may replay it.
Nonsense — speech that is largely impossible to understand because of word salad, broken word fragments, severely malformed sentences, or random word combinations that carry no meaning. Do not judge factual accuracy, relevance, voice quality, or repetition unless the words themselves become incomprehensible.
